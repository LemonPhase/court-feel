# Code of Conduct

Court Feel started at a volleyball club and is built by players, coaches and
developers together. Treat each other the way a good team does.

## Where this applies

Everywhere people work on or talk about Court Feel: this repository (issues,
pull requests, reviews, comments) and the club's chats and conversations
about the project.

## What we expect

* Be kind and patient, especially with people new to code or new to
  volleyball. Every level of player is welcome, and so is every level of
  programmer.
* Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This set arrives too late for
  a go" helps; "this is rubbish" doesn't.
* Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
* Accept that the maintainer's decision is final, and expect them to explain
  it against the [principle](README.md#the-principle).
* Respect people's privacy. Don't share screenshots, clips or messages that
  show other people without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

* Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
* Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's gender, sexuality,
  ethnicity, religion, disability, age, body or level of skill.
* Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
* Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
* Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or ignoring a request to stop.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks this code, tell the maintainer,
[LemonPhase](https://github.com/LemonPhase), privately: in person at the club
or through any private channel you have with them. Reports are kept
confidential. If the maintainer is involved, tell a club committee member
instead.

## What happens next

The maintainer will listen, look at what happened and respond in proportion:
a private word, a public warning, a temporary ban from the project's spaces or
a permanent one. Removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, issues and
pull requests that break this code is part of that.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
