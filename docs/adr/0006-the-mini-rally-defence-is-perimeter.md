# The Mini Rally defence is perimeter

The AI teams defend in perimeter, the most cited base system indoors: on
their set the line digger goes to the line, the cross digger (the libero, at
left back) cross court outside the block, the back-row outside deep at
middle back, and the front-row player who isn't blocking pulls off the net
for tips. Middle-middle (the middle back up behind the block) was turned
down as the base; the middle back only steps up onto a seam when the block
leaves one. The libero at left back and the back-row outside at middle back
were MatusGib's call.
