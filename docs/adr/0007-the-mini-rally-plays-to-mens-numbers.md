# The Mini Rally plays to men's numbers

The Mini Rally is tuned against men's statistics (PlusLiga, FIVB): in system
about half to two thirds of attacks are kills, a quick two in three, about one
attack in ten is blocked and 5-8% are hit out, and serves win and lose points.
An attack-friendly balance was chosen over the old one, where the defence and
the passing were close to perfect (18% kills, no aces). The balance is set in
order: the reception first (tougher serves, pass error that grows with the
serve's speed), then the dig (shanks), then the attack (the open spot, short
quicks, fast sets, using the block). The rules of the touch are the same for
the user and the AI: the extra reach at a hard ball and the shank rule are not
an AI bonus or an AI handicap.
